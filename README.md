# What is Belarusian Translations?
This is my personal resource pack that includes translations of dozens of mods into Belarusian. The project is continuously updated and expanded with new mods. You can also contribute by submitting your own translation, or — if you are a mod developer — request a translation for official support of your mod.

<details>
<summary>How to install our translations</summary>
  
1. Download any version of our resource pack to your PC.

2. Without extracting, place the archive into your game's resourcepacks folder. Default path: ``C:\Users\User\AppData\Roaming\.minecraft\resourcepacks``.

3. In the game, open Settings → Resource Packs and enable our resource pack from the left list so it appears in the right list. IMPORTANT: our resource pack must be above all others to work correctly.

</details>

It's important to understand that I'm currently working alone and all translations are done personally by me without anyone's help. That's why I would be very grateful for any assistance. You can contribute on [ParaTranz](https://paratranz.cn/projects/19252) or GitHub (i'm working on that one right now).
